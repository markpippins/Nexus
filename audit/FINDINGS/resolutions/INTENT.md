# FINDINGS / Resolutions — Intent

**Established:** 2026-06-20

This directory records resolutions to Open Questions raised by harvested chat transcripts.
When DeepSeek V4 or other models surface an Open Question that can be answered by existing
specs in the audit corpus, the resolution is recorded here with cross-references.

**Resolution pattern:**
1. Source: harvested document + candidate title
2. Question: from the Open Questions section
3. Answer: resolved by reference to canonical spec(s) in CROSS_REFERENCES.md
4. Status: Resolved | Unresolved | Requires Discussion

For examples, see `example-from-session-2026-06-20.md`.
