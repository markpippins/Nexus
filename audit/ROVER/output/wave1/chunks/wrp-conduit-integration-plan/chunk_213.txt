# 57. Invariants

The following invariants apply to the Conduit → Nebula WRP projection model.

## I1. Nebula is the authoritative long-form memory for WRP

Conduit may track execution, but architectural WRP meaning must remain represented in Nebula.

## I2. Every WRP artifact must have a stable artifact key

Paths may change; artifact identity must not.

## I3. Every Nebula document and chunk must have a deterministic effective level

A chunk either declares a level or inherits from its document.

## I4. Cross-reference edges are level-neutral by default

The source and target nodes carry the abstraction semantics.

## I5. The initial WRP packet must be explicitly cross-linked

Protocol, DAG, traversal, and migration documents must not exist as isolated documents.

## I6. Role-aware retrieval must filter by level before broad cross-reference expansion

Otherwise the stratification model loses value.

## I7. Execution traceability must be additive, not destructive

Implementation evidence and receipts should enrich architectural records rather than overwrite them.
