InvariantsThe following invariants apply to the Conduit → Nebula WRP projection model.I1. Nebula is the authoritative long-form memory for WRPConduit may track execution, but architectural WRP meaning must remain represented in Nebula.I2. Every WRP artifact must have a stable artifact keyPaths may change; artifact identity must not.I3. Every Nebula document and chunk must have a deterministic effective levelA chunk either declares a level or inherits from its document.I4. Cross-reference edges are level-neutral by defaultThe source and target nodes carry the abstraction semantics.I5. The initial WRP packet must be explicitly cross-linkedProtocol, DAG, traversal, and migration documents must not exist as isolated documents.I6. Role-aware retrieval must filter by level before broad cross-reference expansionOtherwise the stratification model loses value.I7. Execution traceability must be additive, not destructiveImplementation evidence and receipts should enrich architectural records rather than overwrite them.58.
