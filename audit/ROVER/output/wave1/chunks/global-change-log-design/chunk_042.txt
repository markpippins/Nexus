# Nexus Topology Self-Model (TSM) — Schema Sketch v0.1

## 0. Design goals

The TSM should let Nexus representboth structure and interpretation.

It needs to capture:

- Inventory— what components/artifacts/systems exist
Inventory— what components/artifacts/systems exist

- Relationships— how they depend on or use each other
Relationships— how they depend on or use each other

- Interpretation— what each thing isforand whether it isconstitutive,optional,external,experimental, etc.
Interpretation— what each thing isforand whether it isconstitutive,optional,external,experimental, etc.

- Evidence / provenance— whether a claim came from code scan, docs, chat, steward assertion, or manual curation
Evidence / provenance— whether a claim came from code scan, docs, chat, steward assertion, or manual curation

- Temporal state— “what Nexus believes right now” as well as “what it believed at snapshot X”
Temporal state— “what Nexus believes right now” as well as “what it believed at snapshot X”

- Compatibility / bridge hints— which artifacts are likely to survive into Assistance and in what role
Compatibility / bridge hints— which artifacts are likely to survive into Assistance and in what role

That means the model needsthree layers:

- Topology Layer— nodes, interfaces, relationships, runtime placements
Topology Layer— nodes, interfaces, relationships, runtime placements

- Interpretation Layer— role of component, purpose, identity/core/optional classification
Interpretation Layer— role of component, purpose, identity/core/optional classification

- Evidence Layer— observations, assertions, confidence, source provenance, snapshot context
Evidence Layer— observations, assertions, confidence, source provenance, snapshot context
