# 22. Projection Sources

A WRP artifact may enter Nebula from one of three sources.

## Source A — Markdown-authored spec

The preferred source. A curated markdown spec exists under the repo and is imported into Nebula.

## Source B — Conduit plan projection

A Conduit plan or work packet is projected into Nebula as a lightweight execution-facing document when no richer Nebula document exists yet.

## Source C — Manual or generated synthesis

A generated or manually synthesized architecture note is imported and later normalized.
